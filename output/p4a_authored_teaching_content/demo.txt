Concept Explanation
Standard deviation is a measure of how spread out the values in a dataset are around the mean. It is measured in the same units as the original observations.

Visual Comparison
Compare the supplied datasets: same mean, different spread.
Show both datasets around a marked mean of 10. Dataset B is more dispersed than Dataset A. Use the original observations as plotted points.

Calculation Method
Determine the mean. Determine each observation's deviation from the mean. Square the deviations. Average the squared deviations. Take the square root.

Summary Statistics Method
Use the supplied number of observations, their sum, and the sum of their squares in the summary-statistics relationship. Take the square root of the resulting population variance.
